# WishUpon Color Palette — "Handmade Boutique"

| Role              | Hex       | Usage                                      |
|--------------------|-----------|---------------------------------------------|
| Background         | `#FAF7F5` | Page background — warm cream/off-white     |
| Primary            | `#5B3A7A` | Logo violet — headers, nav, logo area      |
| Accent             | `#C9A9E0` | Soft lilac — buttons, hover states         |
| Text               | `#2A1B3D` | Deep plum — body text (not pure black)     |
| Secondary/Neutral  | `#D9C9B8` | Warm beige — card backgrounds, borders     |

## Notes
- Background is warm/light on purpose — makes product photography (beads, metal)
  look natural and inviting, rather than clinical.
- Use Primary (#5B3A7A) for the logo area, nav bar, and main CTAs.
- Use Accent (#C9A9E0) for hover states and secondary buttons.
- Avoid pure black (#000000) and pure white (#FFFFFF) for body text/backgrounds —
  use Text and Background colors above instead, for a softer "handmade" feel.
